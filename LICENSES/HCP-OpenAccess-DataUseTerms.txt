WU-Minn HCP Consortium Open Access Data Use Terms
Version 2013-Apr-26
Full text: https://www.humanconnectome.org/study/hcp-young-adult/document/wu-minn-hcp-consortium-open-access-data-use-terms

These terms govern the fs_LR standard surfaces, sulcal-depth maps, medial-wall masks, registration
spheres, and the Glasser HCP-MMP1.0 parcellation redistributed by cortical_maps (derived from
HCP / Van Essen group data — the fs_LR 32k Glasser file is a conversion of the official HCP dlabel,
NOT the fsaverage CC-BY figshare mirror, so the HCP Data Use Terms apply).

Key clauses (verbatim):

  4. I may redistribute original WU-Minn HCP Open Access data and any derived data as long as
     the data are redistributed under these same Data Use Terms.

  5. I will acknowledge the use of WU-Minn HCP data and data derived from WU-Minn HCP data when
     publicly presenting any results or algorithms that benefitted from their use.
     a. ... "Data were provided [in part] by the Human Connectome Project, WU-Minn Consortium
        (Principal Investigators: David Van Essen and Kamil Ugurbil; 1U54MH091657) funded by the
        16 NIH Institutes and Centers that support the NIH Blueprint for Neuroscience Research;
        and by the McDonnell Center for Systems Neuroscience at Washington University."

Redistribution is therefore PERMITTED provided these same terms travel with the data and the
acknowledgment above is included in any publication. Please also cite:
  Van Essen et al. 2012, Cerebral Cortex (10.1093/cercor/bhr291)
  Glasser et al. 2013, NeuroImage (10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.04.127)
